About DMM Woodworking

Rooted in tradition. Built on skill and integrity.

DMM Woodworking specializes in custom kitchens, closet systems, vanities, wet and dry bars and other fine woodworking projects. We design, manufacture and install our own work with uncompromising attention to detail and heirloom-quality craftsmanship for residential and commercial clients.

The Heart of Our Work

Integrity, quality, legacy.

Integrity. From design to relationships, we strive to do everything with integrity.

Quality. We build, and expect our customers to experience, great quality in our product.

Legacy. Business, relationships and our product are built on decisions to leave a lasting impact in this generation and beyond.

We strive to provide a seamless process from initial contact to the end of installation. We start with reviewing the goals you have with your space, and work these into a final design. Throughout design and material selections, we utilise 3D renderings to show you, as accurately as possible, what the finished space will look like. Once approved, the project moves out to the shop where our team builds and assembles the cabinets. We then deliver and install the cabinets, trim, and hardware.

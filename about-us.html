About DMM Woodworking

Rooted in tradition. Built on skill and integrity.

DMM Woodworking specializes in custom kitchens, closet systems, vanities, wet and dry bars and other fine woodworking projects. We design, manufacture and install our own work with uncompromising attention to detail and heirloom-quality craftsmanship for residential and commercial clients.

Our Story

Concept to completion, under one roof.

We've been Lancaster's custom cabinet makers since 1993. Every piece we create reflects a commitment to excellent materials, honest communication and timeless craftsmanship.

Because design, manufacturing and installation all happen in-house, the people who draw your cabinets are the same people who build and hang them — so nothing gets lost in translation.

We partner with custom home builders, designers, architects and homeowners to create custom, quality cabinetry that elevates every space.

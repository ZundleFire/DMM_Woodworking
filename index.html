About DMM Woodworking

Lancaster's custom cabinet makers since 1993.

We design, manufacture and install our own work — custom kitchens, closet systems, vanities, wet and dry bars, bedroom sets and fine furniture — with uncompromising attention to detail and heirloom-quality craftsmanship for residential and commercial clients.

From the first sketch to the final piece of hardware, every project is handled under one roof by the people who build it.

What We Do

Design. Build. Install.

We design, build and install custom cabinet solutions for kitchens, bathrooms, closets, mudrooms and any other space in the home.

Step 01

Design

We work with you from the start to understand how you actually use your space, then fully design each cabinet and use photo-realistic renderings so you can see the beauty and function before we build a thing.

Step 02

Build

Once the design and material selections are finalized, we build and finish every cabinet in our Lancaster shop, with rigorous quality control at each stage.

Step 03

Install

Our team delivers and installs the cabinets, trim and hardware — finished seamlessly and ready to use.

Also Available

Custom doors, drawer fronts & drawer boxes

Do your doors and drawers need an upgrade? We build custom solid-wood drawer boxes in any size and height, along with doors and drawer fronts. It's a popular way to get a completely new look without replacing all of the cabinetry.
